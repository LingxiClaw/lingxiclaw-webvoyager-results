# Lingxi AI Assistant on WebVoyager

**87.15% with seven execution-error retries and 86.02% on the initial dispatch.** LingxiClaw succeeded on 536 of 615 tasks in the final November 2026 evaluation, including seven that succeeded on a later execution. The initial dispatch accounted for 529 successes. Both scores include every selected task.

China Mobile (Hangzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd (中移（杭州）信息技术有限公司) reports these results for its paid, closed-source Lingxi AI Assistant. The November test used a date-adjusted 615-task subset across 15 websites, a limit of 40 counted browser host calls per execution, and a custom offline Doubao judge. The November report documents the attempt policy and includes the public execution and judge evidence. The other files at repository root document the October evaluation.
